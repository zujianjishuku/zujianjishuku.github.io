Privacy Policy

Football GD Archive (“the application”) is a personal-use archive utility operated by its account owner. This policy describes how the application handles Google Drive data and authorization information.

Information accessed

With the owner's consent, the application uses the Google Drive API https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope to create and manage its own archive files and folders, or files the owner explicitly grants it access to. The data may include football match statistics, source records, file names, file identifiers, file sizes, and integrity hashes.

How information is used

Google Drive data is used solely to upload, organize, retrieve, verify, and, when authorized, manage the owner's archive files. It is not used for advertising, profiling, data brokerage, or training generalized AI models.

Storage and access

Archive files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials, including the refresh token, are stored on an access-restricted system controlled by the application owner. The application does not intentionally publish archive contents or OAuth credentials through this website.

Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data or intentionally share Google API data with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Google Drive remains the storage provider, subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Archived files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them. Local authentication credentials remain until replaced, revoked, or deleted. The owner may revoke access at any time in the Google Account third-party connections or security settings, and remove application-created files from Google Drive.

This website

This informational site does not provide a login or collect information through forms. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which may process standard access information under GitHub's own privacy policy.

Contact

Privacy or application questions can be submitted through the project contact page. Do not post confidential information or credentials in public issues.

归档内容保存在账户所有者的 Google Drive，OAuth 凭据保存在访问受限的私有系统。数据不用于广告、出售个人信息或训练通用 AI 模型，不通过本说明网站公开私有归档内容。
